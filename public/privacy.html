Privacy policy

Prepline · last updated November 2026

The short version

Prepline has no accounts and no analytics. Your plans, recipes, prices, pantry and settings are stored only on your device. We don't collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (budget, store, diet, household size, appliances, foods you skip, ZIP code).
  • Your weekly plans, grocery list ticks, pantry, edited prices, added ingredients and your own recipes.

This data lives in your browser's or app's local storage. Deleting the app or clearing site data removes it. You can export a backup file from Settings at any time.

Optional network requests

  • Daily price files. The app downloads public price files (for example prices/community.json) from the same site it was installed from. No personal data is sent.
  • Price server (optional). If a price server is configured, the app sends your ZIP code and a list of ingredient IDs to get nearby store prices. When you tap "See live prices on Instacart", your grocery list (item names and amounts) is sent so a shopping-list link can be created. The price server keeps short-lived caches of prices and links and doesn't log personal data.
  • Instacart. If you open an Instacart link, Instacart's own privacy policy applies on their site.

Data sources

Shopper-reported prices come from Open Prices by Open Food Facts, available under the Open Database License (ODbL). Store names and logos belong to their owners and are used only to identify where prices apply. Prepline is not affiliated with any retailer.

Children

Prepline isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
